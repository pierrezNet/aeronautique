Phraseology

Practise VFR and IFR radio communications, in French and English: three levels, scored quiz, guided full flight, an ATC that talks to you and spoken replies. Texts follow the French DGAC phraseology manual.

UTC watch

A clock set to UTC that you can embed in your website. It uses motion and light sensors to give you a playful experience.

SELCAL

SELCAL is a selective calling system that lets a ground operator alert an aircraft crew that they wish to talk to them. Each aircraft is assigned a 4-letter code made of two audio dual tones.

Open the app

Settings Workshop

Understand what limits your frames per second in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024: bottleneck diagnosis, a target that suits your display, sourced settings, challenge mode and a test journal. Everything stays in your browser.
