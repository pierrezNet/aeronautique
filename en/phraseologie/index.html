Aviation phraseology

A simulator to practise radio communications between pilot and controller, in VFR and IFR, in French and English: read, listen, then reply out loud.

Open the app Forum

Who is it for?

For virtual pilots who fly online with a controller (IVAO, VATSIM…) and want to use correct phraseology, for those new to the radio and for those who want to brush up. The app is designed for flight simulation: it replaces neither training nor the official manual.

What you will find

  • 🛫

    The whole flight, phase by phase

    Start-up, taxi, departure, cruise, approach, landing, with your own settings: call sign, aerodromes, frequencies, runway, transponder.

  • 🌍

    VFR and IFR, French and English

    Two sets of flight rules and two languages, each with its specifics (altitude and exit point in VFR, flight level, SID and STAR in IFR).

  • 📶

    Three levels

    Beginner for a standard flight, intermediate for common situations (traffic, CTOT, go-around…), advanced for rare cases and coordination.

  • 🌦️

    Real weather

    The aerodrome's METAR is fetched in real time: QNH, wind and visibility appear directly in the exchanges.

  • 🎯

    Scored quiz

    The pilot's line is hidden: it is up to you to say it. An answer is accepted if the critical items are right (call sign, runway, QNH, frequencies, levels…) and the score reaches 75%, with a debrief of phraseology mistakes.

  • ✈️

    Guided full flight

    A flight from start to finish, from start-up to parking: instructions at each step, unexpected events, two attempts per line, ATC follow-ups and a review for each phase.

  • 🎧

    ATC talks to you

    Every line can be played with a radio effect. Numbers are spoken as on the frequency: “heading two seven zero”, “tree”, “fife”, “niner”, and in French “unité”.

  • 🎙️

    Reply out loud

    Hold the space bar like a push-to-talk switch (or the button on mobile), read back, release: your message is transcribed and scored straight away.

  • 🗺️

    Traffic circuit map

    In beginner VFR, an interactive map of the traffic circuit leads to the right phraseology at each point of the circuit.

How it works

  1. Enter your flight in the settings: call sign, aerodromes, frequencies, runway, ICAO code for the weather.
  2. Choose a phase of the flight, then a situation: the exchanges are shown with your data.
  3. Practise: read and listen, then switch to the quiz or start a full flight and reply out loud.

Reference phraseology

The texts follow the phraseology manual of the French civil aviation authority (DGAC) (10th edition, 15 April 2023, 2.6 MB PDF, in French) and its latest changes: “plus haut / plus bas”, R_N_P, three-digit headings… In French, the pilot never repeats expressions reserved for the controller: they say “je décolle”, not “autorisé décollage”.

All the texts form an open reference set, published as JSON files on GitHub, as are the pronunciation rules of the voice.

Free, no account needed

The app is free, with no ads and no account to create, and its texts are under an open licence (CC BY 4.0). The voice has a real cost: every spoken line and every transcribed reply goes through a pay-per-use artificial intelligence service. To keep it free, each user has a daily quota (about 200 spoken replies or 500 lines played), renewed every night, within a shared overall cap. The texts, the written quiz and the full flight are always available.

Your data

Your flight settings stay on your device. Without the voice, only the ICAO code for the weather leaves your browser. The text of the lines played and your voice recordings are processed only for the time needed for speech synthesis or transcription; recordings are not kept. Details are in the app's “About” section.

The frequency is yours

A remark on phraseology, an idea, a bug? The phraseology forum (free to read, sign up to post) and the app's feedback button, no account needed, are there for that.
